Professional risk sizing: each trade risks a % of equity (not a fixed $), scaled up on higher-conviction setups but never past the hard cap. Fees + slippage are charged on every trade so results are realistic. A daily-loss breaker halts a book after a bad day; a correlation cap limits same-direction positions.
